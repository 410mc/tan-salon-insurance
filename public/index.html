Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about payment, liability, property, umbrella and auto coverage.

General

What payment plans are available?

Premiums can be paid by automatic bank draft on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual basis.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept Mastercard, Visa, Discover and American Express.

What is the difference between an additional insured and a loss payee?

An additional insured, such as your landlord, is added to your liability policy so it is also protected if a claim involves the premises you lease. A loss payee, such as the company that leases you tanning-bed equipment, is named on your property coverage so it is paid for its interest in that equipment if it is damaged or destroyed.

General and Professional Liability

What liability limits do you recommend?

We generally recommend limits starting at $300,000 and going up to an aggregate of $3 million, depending on your business. A quote request lets us match the limits to your situation.

Are booth renters and independent contractors covered?

Booth renters and independent contractors are not your employees, but they can be insured as though they were. They are covered only for work performed at your business.

What is the difference between occurrence and aggregate limits?

The occurrence limit is the most the policy pays for any single claim. The aggregate limit is the most it pays in total for all claims during the policy period.

What does professional liability mean?

Professional liability covers claims that your services caused harm, such as a bad result from a treatment or service you provided to a client.

Is a chemical burn covered?

Yes. A chemical burn from a service you perform is covered under professional liability.

Does a client waiver card protect me from being sued?

No. A waiver card does not stop a client from suing you and does not prevent you from being found liable. Liability insurance is what protects you if that happens.

Are there cancer exclusions?

No. Our tanning coverage has no cancer exclusions.

Property

What property coverage is available?

Coverage is available on a replacement cost basis for your building and contents. Options include business income loss, crime, sign and equipment breakdown coverage.

Umbrella

What does an umbrella policy do?

An umbrella policy protects you against a catastrophic lawsuit by paying above the limit of your general liability or commercial auto liability policy, with limits up to $10,000,000.

Auto

Is commercial auto available?

Yes, commercial auto insurance is available for vehicles used in your business.
